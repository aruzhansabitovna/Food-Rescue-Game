Why rescue food?

Wasting food also wastes the water, energy, and resources used to produce it. This game teaches practical habits: check food before discarding it, store it safely, use leftovers, and plan portions.

💚 Educational game • No real food is saved by in-game actions • Your choices can inspire real-world change.
